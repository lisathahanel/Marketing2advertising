🌐 Jobs @Marketing2Advertising (M2A)

Founded in 2015, Marketing2Advertising (M2A) is a global advertising and marketing agency specializing in internet‑based services. We empower professionals and students by offering accessible, high‑quality advertising, marketing, educational resources, career guidance, and engaging entertainment.

1. Eligibility

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years old or meet the legal working age in their country of residence.

  • International applicants may be required to obtain valid work permits or visa sponsorship before employment.

2. Application Process

  • All applications must be submitted through official M2A channels (our website, email, or approved recruitment partners).
  • Submission of false, misleading, or incomplete information may result in disqualification.
  • M2A reserves the right to verify academic qualifications, work history, and references.

3. Employment Terms

  • Employment offers are subject to successful completion of interviews, background checks, and any required training.
  • Roles, responsibilities, and compensation will be outlined in individual employment contracts.

6. Compensation & Benefits

Salaries and benefits will be provided according to the employment contract and local labor laws. M2A may offer additional perks such as training opportunities, career development programs, and access to educational resources.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate employment in accordance with the notice period specified in the contract. Immediate termination may occur in cases of gross misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or violation of company policies.

M2A is not responsible for any loss or damage arising from misrepresentation by applicants. Applicants and employees are responsible for ensuring compliance with local laws and regulations.
