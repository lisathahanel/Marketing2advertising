How Does Starlink Internet Work?


Starlink uses a constellation of low Earth orbit (LEO) satellites. These satellites are much closer to the Earth than traditional geostationary satellites.


This proximity reduces the time it takes for data to travel from the satellite to the user. As a result, Starlink can offer high-speed internet with low latency.

The satellites communicate with ground stations on Earth. These stations connect to the wider internet, creating a link between the user and the online world.

Interpreting Your Starlink Speed Test Results


Once you've run the speed test, you'll see a set of numbers. But what do these numbers mean?


The download speed is the rate at which data is transferred from the internet to your device. This is important for activities like streaming videos or downloading files.


The upload speed is the rate at which data is transferred from your device to the internet. This is crucial for activities like video calls or uploading files to the cloud.


The ping time, also known as latency, measures the time it takes for data to travel from your device to the server and back. Lower ping times mean less delay and smoother online experiences, especially for real-time activities like online gaming.
