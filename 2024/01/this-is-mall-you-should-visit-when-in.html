Zimbabwe Travel Guide · Harare Shopping

Stores, food, free parking and everything else you need to know before you go

If you're after a modern, easy, low-stress shopping trip in Harare, put Madokero Mall on your list. It sits at the busy corner of Kirkman Road and Dr Nyamutswa Road in the Tynwald suburb, and since it opened in December 2022 it has quickly become a local favorite.

Maybe you're running your weekly errands, hunting for the right home decor, or just looking for a lively place to eat with the family. Madokero Mall covers all of that, and I'll walk you through what you'll find there.

Everything You Need Under One Roof

One of the best things about this mall is the range of stores. You can cross most of your to-do list off in a single visit. Here's how it breaks down.

Groceries and Essentials

Pick n Pay is the mall's big anchor store, stocked with fresh produce and your daily essentials. Right next door is Pick n Pay Liquor, which has you covered for beverages.

The Food Court and Hangout Spots

Whether you want a quick bite or a sit-down meal, you've got plenty of choices, from local and international names like Steers, Chicken Hut, Pogo's Great Pizza & Chicken, All the Way Burgers and Picadilly Pizza. For fresh bread and something sweet, stop by The Bake House or The Candy Hut. And if you like a lively, open-air social scene, Emagumeni is the spot.

Fashion and Home Decor

Refresh your wardrobe at Jet Stores, pick up premium bedding at Zaywa Linen and Linen Central, or upgrade your electronics and furniture at TV Sales & Home.

Health, Wellness and Beauty

You can look after your health with professional eye care at Eye Clinic Opticians, fill prescriptions at MedOrange Pharmacy, or treat yourself to a fresh look at Craig's Hair Studio.

Daily Conveniences

Grab premium cuts at Texas Meats, which also carries Texas Halaal options. Drop off your laundry at Central Dry Cleaners, or send and receive money safely through Mukuru.

Why Locals Love It

The mall was built with convenience in mind, and it shows. Here's what makes a visit easier:

  • A large, secure parking lot with free parking.
  • Fully wheelchair-accessible spaces.
  • A children's playground to keep the little ones busy.
  • A fuel station nearby, which makes it a handy pit stop on the way home.
