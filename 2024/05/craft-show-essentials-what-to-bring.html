Participating in a craft show, especially in scenic locations like Letchworth State Park, can be both an exhilarating and daunting experience for artisans. Preparing for a craft or art show means considering not just your merchandise, but also the practicality and aesthetics of your vendor booth.


Whether it's your first time or you're a seasoned exhibitor, having a checklist of essentials can ensure your booth stands out and provides a memorable experience for visitors.



Display Fixtures and Decorations


Creating an inviting and organized display is crucial. Consider the flow of traffic and how easily customers can view and access your items. Use a variety of fixtures like shelves, racks, and tables to give your booth dimension. Don’t forget to bring covers for tables that reach the ground to hide storage and supplies. Decorate your space with signage, banners, and even some DIY flair to reflect your brand and catch the eye of passersby.


Attention to Detail


Small details can have a big impact. Use price tags that match your brand's aesthetic, and have business cards or flyers available for potential customers to take away. If your craft is small, like jewelry, use magnifying glasses or provide ample lighting to showcase the intricate work.

Inventory and Packaging


Bring a substantial amount of inventory, but also be mindful of overstocking, which can make your booth look cluttered. Have a secure storage solution for extra stock. Ensure you have packaging materials like bags, boxes, and wrapping to safely send your customers home with their new treasures.


Point of Sale System



In today's digital world, cash-only sales can limit your revenue. Bring a mobile point of sale system or card reader, and ensure you have a way to charge these devices throughout the event. Also, keep a cash box with plenty of change for those who prefer to pay with cash.


Comfort and Convenience Items


Art shows like the one in Letchworth State Park often run for several hours or even multiple days. Bring items for personal comfort such as a chair, water, snacks, and weather-appropriate clothing. Consider a canopy or umbrella for shade or shelter, and weights or stakes to secure your setup.


Marketing Materials


Don't miss the opportunity to market your business. Have a newsletter signup sheet or a way to capture emails. Prepare a portfolio or slideshow of your work to display on a tablet or in a binder for interested visitors.


Emergency Kit


Always have an emergency kit on hand with basic tools, tape, scissors, and other repair items. This can be a lifesaver for last-minute fixes to your display or products.


By ensuring you have all the essentials for your craft show booth, you're setting yourself up for success. Participating in shows, particularly in a beautiful setting like Letchworth State Park, is not only a chance to sell but also to connect with fellow artisans and the community. With a well-thought-out booth, you can make a lasting impression that extends beyond the event itself.
