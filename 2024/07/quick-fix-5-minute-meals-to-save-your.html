Spicy Garlic Noodles (5 minutes)

Ingredients:

- 2 servings cooked pasta (your choice)

- 1/4 cup olive oil

- 4 cloves garlic, minced

- 1/2 red chili pepper, thinly sliced (optional)

- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Cook pasta according to package directions.

2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add garlic and chili pepper (if using) and cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant.

3. Drain pasta and toss immediately with the hot oil mixture.

4. Season with salt, pepper, and parsley. Serve hot.

Additional Tips:

Meal Prep Hack: Pre-cook a pot of brown rice or quinoa on Sundays to have a quick base for meals throughout the week.

Nutrient Boost: Keep a bag of frozen vegetables on hand to add an extra burst of nutrition to your meals.

Chicken Transformations: Leftover roasted chicken or shredded rotisserie chicken can be transformed into quick stir-fries or salads.
