Once upon a time…

In a land of dazzling lights and royal romance, there lived a woman named Eve. She wasn’t just beautiful—she was breathtaking. The kind of woman whose face could stop time, whose walk turned heads. Every man in the kingdom longed to marry her. But Eve had a rule:

Never marry an ugly man—even if he owns the moon.

And she meant it.

Meanwhile, in a faraway land, there lived a man named Kenneth. He was the kind of handsome that made women faint. Poets wrote about his jawline. Painters couldn’t do justice to his eyes. Yet, none of the women around him ever felt right—he believed they were all beneath his level of beauty.

One day, Kenneth heard whispers of a mysterious beauty who rejected every man that came near. She thinks no one is good-looking enough for her? he laughed. Challenge accepted.

He packed his royal bags and traveled to meet this mysterious Eve. And when he arrived… the whole country exploded in gossip:

The most handsome man in the world is here!
Has Eve finally met her match?

Even Eve herself, proud as ever, was curious. She asked for an audience. And when Kenneth walked in—tall, proud, glowing like a movie star—Eve looked at him and, for the first time ever, she smiled.

Will you marry me, Eve? Kenneth asked confidently.

Yes, she replied instantly. The crowd gasped.

A Fairytale Gone Wrong
Months passed, and the kingdom awaited the arrival of what everyone expected to be the most beautiful baby ever born.

But when the child came… silence. The baby boy was not what anyone expected.

He was, by their impossible standards, ugly.

Eve was horrified. Kenneth was speechless. Their dreams of a perfect, beautiful family shattered in seconds.

Worried about their reputation, the couple begged the doctor to take the child away and tell the world he had died. That doctor—her name was Merlin—was deeply troubled. She couldn’t bear to harm the baby.

You see, Merlin had prayed for years for a child of her own, but could never have one. So she made a decision:

She took the baby, raised him in secret, and named him… Handsome.

The Rise of Handsome
Years flew by.

Eve and Kenneth—once the golden couple—grew older. Their beauty faded. Their wealth dried up. People no longer cared.

Meanwhile, Handsome, raised with love, grew into a kind-hearted and strikingly good-looking man. His smile could light up a room, and his compassion touched everyone around him.

One day, the country held an election. And guess who the people chose to be their President?

That’s right—Handsome, the child once rejected, forgotten, and left for dead.

The Reunion
After learning the truth from Merlin, President Handsome decided to visit his birth parents. When he arrived at their crumbling home, Eve and Kenneth couldn’t believe their eyes.

You... you’re our son? Eve whispered.

The one we… Kenneth couldn’t finish his sentence.

Handsome smiled. Yes. The one you called ugly. The one you gave away. But I forgive you. Because everyone makes mistakes. And no matter what... you're still my parents.

They hugged. They wept. And from that day forward, they became a family once again—stronger, wiser, and forever changed.

Moral of the Story
True beauty isn't in the face. It's in the heart. And sometimes, the ugly parts of our story are the ones that make us truly beautiful.
