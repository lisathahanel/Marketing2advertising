Just like you, your audience is unique. That's why reaching them with personalized and beautifully designed emails is a key to your success as a creator. With ConvertKit's easy-to-use email editor and customizable templates, you're well on your way to standing out in any inbox.


Let's break down some ConvertKit terminology to help you craft emails that are as captivating as your ideas.


Email templates are more than just reusable formats; they are canvases for your creativity. Personalize them to match your branding or choose from our wide array of professionally designed templates, all without needing to touch a line of code (unless you want to—you can still add custom CSS to our visual editor).


Broadcast emails are your direct line to your audience. Whether it's a one-time message, a weekly newsletter, or a sales campaign, our broadcast tool makes it a breeze to design and send distinctive, non-automated emails.


Sequences automate your email flow, ensuring your fans receive the right content at the right time. These scheduled series of emails, like welcome emails, are simple to design and personalize with our email editor.
