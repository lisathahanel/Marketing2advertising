Mu Valencia, Spain, ku lunaku olw’emmuli, abantu basanvu abalina ebyuma by’obulabe mu mikono gye, abambiddwa mu ngeri y’okubera abalala, basuula ku banki. On a rainy morning, six armed men in disguise attempt a high-stakes bank heist. Ebintu byalabika nga bingiira mu ngeri ey’amaanyi, naye ekikolwa kyokka tekisobola kukyakanya.

Omukwano n’amaanyi g’ebyama bitandika okuba mu kkomo lya buli kimu. What seemed like a straightforward robbery quickly spirals out of control, as nothing unfolds according to plan. Obutaliimu n’okwesalira okufuna ebyama byeyongera okubaamu enteekateeka ey’akabi.
