Omuwala eyali omuvuzi w’emmotoka mu mpaka addamu mu by’empaka ng’ayingira mu academy y’okutendekebwa. There she meets Will, and their shared passion for racing quickly turns into a growing connection. Nga batandika okumanyagana, obulamu bwabwe butandika okukyuka mu ngeri etali yasuubirwa.

Mukwano gwabwe gukula mangu, naye embeera ekyuka bwe bafuuka abavuganya mu mpaka z’amaanyi. They fall in love but soon become rivals competing for the Formula 1 championship. Okusisinkana kwa mukwano n’okuvuganya kuleeta obuzibu mu mitima gyabwe.
