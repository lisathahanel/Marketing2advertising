Jonny ne James, abooluganda ab’ekitundu abaali bawukanye okumala ebbanga, baddamu okwegatta oluvannyuma lw’okufa kwa kitaabwe mu mbeera ez’ekyama. The mysterious death forces the estranged half-brothers to reunite despite their troubled past. Okufa kuno kubazza wamu mu kiseera ekijjudde okubuusabuusa n’ennaku.

Nga batandika okunoonya amazima, basanga ebyama ebyali byakwekebwa okumala emyaka mingi. Their investigation uncovers buried secrets and hidden connections that point to something far more dangerous. Buli kyebazuula kyeyongera okubayingiza mu mukutu ogw’akabi n’obulimba.
