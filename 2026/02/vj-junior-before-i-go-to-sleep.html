Christine Lucas alina obuzibu olw’emyoyo egisanyizo mu mutwe, era alina anterograde amnesia. Ever since a traumatic head injury, she cannot form new memories and has forgotten the last 15 years of her life. Buli lunaku, alina okulaba bba we Ben n’ebintu eby’amaanyi ebirala mu bulamu bwe nga bimu.

Buli lunaku afuna essanyu n’okukiririza mu buzadde n’obwesige bw’abaana n’abantu abalala abamu. Each morning she must reintroduce herself to her husband and the constants in her life, relearning everything. Obulamu bwe bubera mu kkomo ly’okwongera okufuna amazima nga buli kiseera ayita mu mbeera etaliimu.
