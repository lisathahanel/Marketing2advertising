Mu kugenderera okudda ku biseera bye byayita, Millie Calloway akkiriza omulimu ogw’okubeera omukozi w’ennyumba mu maka g’abo abakyala abasinga obugagga, Nina ne Andrew Winchester. She hopes the job will give her a fresh start and stability, leaving her past behind, but what seems perfect at first soon begins to change. Obulamu bwe mu maka g’abo buggya mu ngeri ey’okuyitiriza, nga buli kimu kye akola kiba n’ekizikiza.

Wadde omulimu gulabika nga gwewulira obulamu obulungi, the household’s secrets and hidden agendas quickly surface, turning Millie’s dream job into a dangerous game. Okwekalakaasa, amaanyi ag’akakadde, n’okusisinkana kwebulamu byeyongera okujjaamu obulabe obw’amaanyi. Buli kimu kye akola kisobola okuba nga kiyitibwa mu mukutu ogujjudde obusungu n’okukwatibwa ku ngeri gye tasobola kugenza.

Mu nsi eno ey’akabi n’amaanyi, Millie ayingira mu mukutu ogujjudde amagezi, obulabe n’okwekalakaasa. She must decide whom to trust, navigate manipulation, and survive the deadly intrigues of the household. Omukwano, obwesige, n’amaanyi byeyongera okuba mu kigezo, era buli kimu kye akola kisobola okuleeta obulabe oba okuzza amaanyi mu nsi ey’akabi eyo.
