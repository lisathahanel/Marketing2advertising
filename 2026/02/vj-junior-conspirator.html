Mary Surratt ye mukazi yekka eyali alagiddwa okuba omuwandiisi mu nsonga y’okutwalibwa kwa Abraham Lincoln. As the whole nation turns against her, she finds herself isolated and under immense pressure, with everyone questioning her loyalty. Obulamu bwe bwekityo bweyongera okuba mu kigezo, nga buli kimu kye akola kyeyongera okubuusabuusa n’obutaliimu.

Wadde akubiddwa mu nsi y’ebibuuzo n’obutaliimu, Mary ayingira mu mukutu ogw’okufuna amazima. She must rely on her reluctant lawyer to investigate, navigate political tension, and uncover the truth that could save her life. Okusigala mu nsi eyo ey’obutaliimu n’obuwumbi bw’amaanyi kweyongera okuzza amaanyi mu mbeera ye.
