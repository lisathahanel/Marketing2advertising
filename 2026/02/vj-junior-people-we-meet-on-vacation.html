Poppy alina omutima ogw’obulamu obw’essanyu era ayagala okweyagala mu ngeri y’akakiiko. Alex, on the other hand, loves structure and plans, making him the opposite of Poppy. Nga bali mu myaka mingi gyebafunye emikolo gy’ekkyaaliro, obulamu bwabwe bweyongera okuba mu ngeri ey’okuyitiriza.

Abasajja n’abakazi bano abali ku ngeri ez’enjawulo batandika okufuna obusobozi bw’okukola obulamu bwabwe obw’obulungi. Through years of summer vacations together, they begin to question if their differences could complement each other. Obulamu bwabwe bweyongera okuba mu kkomo ly’okwekalakaasa, obulamu n’eby’omukwano.
