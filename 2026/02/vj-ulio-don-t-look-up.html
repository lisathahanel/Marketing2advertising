Abalondoozi babiri b’eby’emmunyeenye bazuula nti waliwo kometi ennene egenda okukuba ensi n’okugizikiriza. Realizing the planet-killing threat, they launch a desperate media tour to warn humanity before it’s too late. Naye nga bagezaako okulabula ensi yonna, abantu bangi tebalabika nga batwala nsonga eno nga ya maanyi.

Mu nsi ejjudde amawulire, ebyobufuzi n’okwekalakaasa ku mikutu gya social media, obubaka bwabwe butwalibwa ng’ekintu ekitali kya maanyi. Instead of urgent action, they face indifference, political spin, and public distraction. Abantu bangi baddamu mu ngeri ey’okwebuuza obubaka bwabwe, nga balabika ng’abagamba nti, “Meh.”
