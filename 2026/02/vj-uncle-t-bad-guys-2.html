Ekibiina kya Bad Guys, kati nga kyewandiisizza okuba abalungi, bagezaako nnyo (nnyo ddala) okubeera ku ludda olutuufu. After reforming, they are determined to prove they can live honest lives and leave their criminal past behind. Naye obulamu obw’obulungi tebuba bwangu nga bwe baasuubira.

Mu kifo ky’okufuna emirembe, basanga nga bayingiziddwa mu buwamba obw’ekyama mu mpaka enkulu ez’okubbira mu nsi yonna. They are hijacked into a high-stakes, globe-trotting heist that puts their new reputation at risk. Ekikolwa kino kitegekeddwa ekibiina ekipya eky’abamenyi b’amateeka kye batamanyiiko.
