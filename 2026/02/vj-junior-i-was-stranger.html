Ddokita ow’e Syria ne muwala we bakimukira okuva mu Aleppo, nga bateeka mu nsi y’obulamu obutaliimu kifo ekyokka. A Syrian doctor and her daughter flee Aleppo, setting off events that will change many lives. Obulamu bwabwe bubadde mu kkomo ly’okulwanyisa obutaliimu n’okwesalira okutuuka ku bantu ab’enjawulo.

Abantu abatali bamanyi bawa obuyambi, bakutira mu nsi y’ebizibu: omusirikale, omusajja ow’ebigambo, omusajja ow’eby’enjigiriza, ne kaputeni w’abapolisi b’ennyanja. A smuggler, a soldier, a poet, and a coast guard captain are drawn together in a crisis. Buli kimu kyeyongera okukakasa obwesige bwabwe n’okwekalakaasa obulamu bwe.
