Omusajja omuto atwalibwa obutali bumu era alina eby’obulamu eby’enjawulo asabibwa okwetaba mu bureau ey’ekyama. A traumatized young man with physical abnormalities is forced to join a mysterious bureau. Obulamu bwe bubadde mu kkomo ly’obwesige, okwetegeka n’okwekalakaasa obulamu bwe.

Olutalo lwe lutambula lwonna ku nsi lweyongera okubaamu obulabe olw’ento ekitalina mazzi oba amazina. He must confront a disaster spreading across the earth, caused by an unknown and terrifying creature. Obwesige bwe n’emyoyo gy’obulamu byeyongera okuba mu kkomo ly’amaanyi n’okwonoona.
