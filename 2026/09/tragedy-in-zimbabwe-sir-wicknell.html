TRAGEDY IN ZIMBABWE: SIR WICKNELL CONFIRMED DEAD

The controversial billionaire, his wife Lucy Muteke, and four others perish in a fiery crash.

The African business and social media landscapes have been thrown into absolute shock following the sudden and tragic death of controversial Zimbabwean billionaire and philanthropist, Wicknell Munodaani Chivayo, popularly known to millions as "Sir Wicknell."

Initial details pouring out from emergency services and state media outlets paint a devastating picture of the incident that occurred on the evening of Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

When and Where Did It Happen?

According to updates released by the Zimbabwe Republic Police (ZRP) and national broadcaster ZBC, the fatal incident occurred at approximately 5:00 PM local time (17:00 CAT). The private helicopter went down in the Marondera Rural area, a district located roughly 70 kilometers east of the capital city, Harare.

How Did the Crash Occur?

While aviation investigators are still working to determine the exact technical or environmental cause of the disaster, government officials have confirmed that the aircraft suffered a complete and catastrophic impact.

Government spokesperson Nick Mangwana and police representatives stated that there were no survivors among the occupants on board, which included two pilots and four passengers. Tragically, first responders noted that due to the severe nature of the impact and a resulting post-crash fire, the bodies recovered from the wreckage were badly burned, necessitating formal forensic identification procedures before official government listings could be finalized.

Family Confirms the Worst

Despite early chaos on social media platforms, family spokesperson Mr. Herbert Chivayo formally confirmed the devastating news to the public. He verified that both Wicknell Chivayo and his well-known wife, Lucy “Lulu” Muteke, were among the casualties, stating that the family is entirely at a loss for words over the sudden double tragedy.

A Flamboyant Legacy Cut Short

Sir Wicknell, 43, was widely recognized as one of the most polarizing yet fascinating public figures in Southern Africa. Rising from humble beginnings as a teenage wages clerk, he built a massive empire under Intratrek Zimbabwe and various regional energy, logistics, and ICT operations.

He was equally famous for his fierce alignment with the ruling ZANU-PF party, his historical public legal battles—from which he was acquitted in recent years—and his jaw-dropping online philanthropy. In the months leading up to his death, his name dominated regional timelines for single-handedly gifting fleets of luxury vehicles, including Range Rovers and Rolls-Royces, to local artists, musicians, and apostolic church members.
