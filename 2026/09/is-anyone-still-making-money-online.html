Let’s start with a question that keeps coming up everywhere online: is anyone still making real money online?

You see people talking about YouTube channels, blogs, affiliate marketing, newsletters, online businesses, and digital products. But at the same time, you also hear creators saying that everything is too saturated, algorithms are getting harder, and the days of easy online money are over.

So, what is actually happening?

Well, people are still making money online. But the opportunity has changed.

The days when someone could upload a few random videos, get millions of views, and suddenly become successful are much harder to replicate. YouTube has become incredibly competitive, and creating quality video content can require cameras, microphones, editing software, thumbnails, research, scripting, and—perhaps most importantly—a huge amount of time.

Instead of depending entirely on video views, you can build a library of useful articles that attracts visitors through search engines. One article might bring visitors today, tomorrow, next month, and potentially much longer if it continues to rank and remain useful.
