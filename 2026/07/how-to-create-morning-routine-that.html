A strong morning routine sets the tone for the entire day. The secret is keeping it simple and consistent.

1. Wake Up at the Same Time

Consistency trains your body’s internal clock, making mornings easier.

2. Hydrate First

Drinking water right after waking up helps refresh your system.

3. Move for 10 Minutes

Stretching or light exercise boosts energy and focus.

4. Plan Your Day

Write down three priorities. This keeps you focused and productive.

Final Thought: Small rituals build big results. Stick with it and mornings will feel effortless.
