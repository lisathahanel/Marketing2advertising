Overview of the 1964 Triumph TR4

The 1964 Triumph TR4 stands as an iconic representation of British automotive engineering and design. Launched during a period marked by an increasing interest in sporty roadsters, the TR4 quickly gained popularity among car enthusiasts and casual drivers alike. Its production from 1961 to 1965 marked a significant chapter for Triumph, positioning the TR4 as a beloved model within the classic car community. The combination of sleek aesthetics and performance capabilities made the TR4 not just a vehicle, but a symbol of the era.

Designed by the renowned Italian automobile designer Giovanni Michelotti, the TR4 showcases a formula that marries performance with elegance. Its appearance is characterized by a long hood, short rear deck, and a distinctive front grille, all of which contribute to its sporty and aggressive stance. This design philosophy not only appealed to the visual sensibilities of the time but also ensured aerodynamic efficiency, which contributed positively to driving dynamics. The integration of a modern (for its time) chassis allowed the TR4 to deliver an engaging driving experience that further bolstered its charm.

In terms of performance, the 1964 Triumph TR4 was equipped with a 2.2-liter four-cylinder engine capable of producing 105 horsepower, a significant leap from its predecessor, the TR3. This enhancement, coupled with its lightweight construction, permitted nimble handling and quick acceleration, making it a favorite on both road and track. The TR4's blend of performance and style resonated especially well with car enthusiasts, fostering a culture of appreciation that persists to this day. Thus, as we delve deeper into the specifications of the 1964 Triumph TR4, we see that this classic British roadster has rightfully earned its place in the pantheon of legendary automobiles.

Detailed Specifications of the 1964 Triumph TR4

The 1964 Triumph TR4 is a classic British sports car that continues to captivate enthusiasts with its timeless design and robust performance. This model, referenced with the number CFJ9206961 and carrying the model code TR4, showcases a unique blend of style and engineering. The Triumph TR4 was officially registered in 1964, marking the beginning of a celebrated automotive journey.

Under the hood, the Triumph TR4 boasts a powerful engine with a capacity of 2000 cc, offering an engaging driving experience. The engine is designed to run on petrol, making it suitable for a variety of driving conditions. With a manual transmission system, it provides drivers with greater control over gear selection, further enhancing the car's sporty appeal. The mileage of this model stands at 12,700 km, indicating a well-maintained vehicle that is ready for both collector showcases and weekend drives alike.

In terms of physical dimensions, the 1964 Triumph TR4 measures 4,029 mm in length, 1,600 mm in width, and 1,270 mm in height. These dimensions contribute to its compact yet sporty stature, tailored for nimble handling and agility on the road. The design features two seats, accommodating driver and passenger in a streamlined cockpit that highlights the car’s racing pedigree. Additionally, the model is equipped with two doors, emphasizing accessibility while retaining its classic sports car look.

Moreover, the TR4 is built on a robust chassis that supports a combination of performance and comfort. With its solid construction and thoughtful engineering, it remains a notable entry in Triumph's legacy of sports cars, appealing to both vintage car collectors and driving enthusiasts. Overall, the specifications of the 1964 Triumph TR4 underline its status as a distinguished vehicle in the automotive world.

Performance and Driving Features

The 1964 Triumph TR4 is recognized for its remarkable performance attributes, particularly due to its robust roadster engine. The TR4 is equipped with a 2.0-liter inline-four engine, generating an impressive 100 horsepower and 120 lb-ft of torque. This engine, combined with a responsive four-speed manual transmission, allows the TR4 to accelerate with commendable agility. Moreover, the addition of Weber carburetors enhances fuel delivery, ensuring optimal engine performance and providing a more spirited driving experience.

The driving experience of the TR4 is one of its striking facets. These cars offer a smooth ride, thanks to their independent front suspension and a live rear axle, which contribute to impressive handling capability. Driver feedback is another hallmark of the TR4; the well-weighted steering system provides a direct connection between the driver and the road, heightening the overall enjoyment of driving. Enthusiasts often praise the car’s ability to navigate corners with confidence, further accentuated by its lightweight construction and balanced chassis design.

Many owners opt for modifications to enhance the TR4's performance. One notable upgrade is the wide body conversion, which not only improves aesthetics but also provides additional stability at higher speeds and while cornering. This modification allows for a wider stance, accommodating broader tires that increase grip and handling. Compared to contemporaneous models, such as the MG B and the Alfa Romeo Spider, the TR4 stands out for its combination of traditional British roadster charm and competitive engine output. While all these vehicles have their strengths, the TR4's unique blend of performance features continues to appeal to classic car enthusiasts today.

Restoration and Collectibility of the TR4

The 1964 Triumph TR4 is a notable model within the realm of classic cars, particularly admired for its appealing design and solid performance. Restoration of this vehicle entails meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that the integrity of its original specifications is maintained. For enthusiasts and collectors, investing time and resources into restoring a TR4 is often worth the effort, given its potential for appreciation in value and status as a collectible classic.

One of the distinguishing factors that contribute to the desirability of the 1964 TR4 is its exterior color. A silver finish not only enhances the car's aesthetic appeal, but it also plays a significant role in its auction grade. Cars that are exquisitely restored and presented in popular colors like silver often command higher bids at auctions, making them more valuable to collectors. The color accentuates the vehicle's lines and curves, making it a standout in any car show or private collection.

The importance of the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) or chassis number cannot be overstated when it comes to classic cars like the Triumph TR4. This number serves as a definitive identifier of the vehicle, providing critical information about its origin, production year, and authenticity. For collectors, verifying the VIN is an essential step to ensure the car is not only genuine but also has a verifiable history, which can significantly enhance its collectibility. Additionally, a well-documented restoration process that includes VIN tracking tends to be more appealing to potential buyers and collectors.

Ultimately, the enduring allure of the 1964 Triumph TR4 lies in its blend of craftsmanship, performance, and aesthetics. Its iconic design, coupled with the allure of restoration and the significance of collected history, ensures that this vehicle remains a cherished piece among car collectors and enthusiasts alike. With each restoration, the TR4 retains its status as a meaningful and enjoyable automotive classic.
