Detailed Specifications

The 2015 Yanmar Vio45-6 is a compact yet powerful machine designed for versatility and efficiency in various applications. The heart of this mini-excavator is its robust engine with a capacity of 4 cylinders and a net power output of 36.4 kW (49.0 hp) at 2,400 RPM. This allows the Vio45-6 to perform heavy digging and lifting tasks with ease, making it suitable for both construction and landscaping operations.

In terms of transmission, the Yanmar Vio45-6 is equipped with a hydrostatic transmission system, which provides smooth and responsive control, essential for precise movements on the job site. This transmission not only enhances maneuverability but also ensures minimal maintenance requirements, extending the overall lifespan of the machine.

Fuel type for the Vio45-6 is diesel, which is characterized by its efficiency and power. Its fuel tank has a capacity of 40 liters, enabling extended operational periods without the need for frequent refueling. The fuel consumption is commendable, helping reduce operating costs over time.

The overall dimensions of the Yanmar Vio45-6 are 4,470 mm in length, 1,960 mm in width, and a height of 2,540 mm, making it compact enough to fit into tight spaces yet substantial enough to tackle significant tasks. The machine boasts a maximum digging depth of 3,260 mm, with a digging radius of 5,735 mm, allowing for a wide work range.

The seating capacity of the Vio45-6 accommodates one operator, optimized for comfort and control with ergonomic design features. Additionally, it usually receives an auction grade indicative of its condition; various grades are assessed based on usage, maintenance, and overall performance history.
