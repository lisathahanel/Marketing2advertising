Minecraft is a game about breaking and placing blocks. At first, players built structures to protect themselves from monsters, but over time it evolved into a world of creativity, adventure, and endless possibilities.

Explore vast landscapes, build incredible structures, survive dangerous nights, and adventure with friends. Whether you're watching the sun rise over a blocky ocean or battling creatures in the Nether, Minecraft offers a unique experience every time you play.

🔥 Game Features

  • Full Minecraft Experience: Build, explore, and survive in an open world.
  • No Downloads Required: Play directly in your browser.
  • Cross-Platform: Works on PC, Mac, Chromebooks, phones, and tablets.
  • Multiplayer Support: Play with friends online.
  • Creative & Survival Modes: Choose your play style.

🎮 Which Mode Should You Choose?

  • WASM-GC (Default): Uses WebAssembly for better performance and smoother gameplay. Recommended for modern devices.
  • Standard JS: Compatibility mode for older devices. Lower FPS but more stable if the game fails to load.

🕹️ How to Play

  • Use WASD to move your character.
  • Use the mouse to look around and interact.
  • Left-click to break blocks, right-click to place them.
  • Open inventory and craft tools to survive.
