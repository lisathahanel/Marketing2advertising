Steps to Check Your Airtel Ugandan Number

If you want to know how to check your Airtel Ugandan number, the process is straightforward. You just need to follow these steps:

  • Open your phone's dialer app.
  • Enter the code *100*10*3*1#
  • Press the call button.

In a few moments, you will see a message on your screen displaying your Airtel Ugandan number. This service is quick and efficient.
