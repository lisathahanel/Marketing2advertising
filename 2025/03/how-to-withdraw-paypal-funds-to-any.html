How to Withdraw PayPal Funds Using Wise (and Payoneer as an Alternative)

Are you experiencing withdrawal restrictions with PayPal? You’re not alone. Many users struggle to access their money due to country-specific limitations or complicated withdrawal processes. Fortunately, there’s a practical solution: using Wise (formerly TransferWise) to move your PayPal funds effortlessly. In this guide, we’ll walk you step by step through creating a Wise account, linking it to PayPal, and transferring your money into your local bank. Plus, we’ll touch on Payoneer as a strong alternative option.

Are You Tired of PayPal's Withdrawal Restrictions?

If you’ve ever tried to withdraw PayPal funds only to hit a wall due to restrictions, it can feel frustrating and limiting. But the good news is that there are workarounds that actually work. Below, you’ll find a simple step-by-step guide to free yourself from these limits and access your money smoothly.

Short on time? Watch the quick video summary at the top of this post for the key takeaways!

Step 1: Create a Wise Account

First, head over to the Wise website and sign up for an account. The setup is straightforward—just provide your details and submit the required documents for verification. Be sure everything is accurate to avoid delays during approval.

Step 2: Request a USD Account

To receive PayPal withdrawals, you’ll need a USD account from Wise. Request one within your Wise dashboard. Sometimes Wise asks for a small deposit (around $15–$20) for verification purposes. Don’t worry—this money is still yours, and you can withdraw it later. This step is crucial because it ensures your account is ready to receive PayPal transactions.

Step 3: Link Wise to PayPal

Now comes the fun part—connect your Wise USD account to PayPal. Once linked, you’ll be able to withdraw funds directly into Wise, bypassing the usual PayPal restrictions. The process is quick and seamless.

Step 4: Transfer Funds to Your Local Bank

After your funds arrive in your Wise USD account, simply transfer them to your local bank account. Wise automatically handles the currency conversion, saving you the headache. Within a short time, your PayPal funds will land in your local account in your own currency.

Alternative Option: Payoneer

If Wise isn’t the right fit for you, Payoneer is another excellent option. It works in a very similar way, allowing you to link your account with PayPal and withdraw funds without hassle. We’ll be covering Payoneer in greater detail in future posts, so keep an eye out!

Final Thoughts

With Wise, you can say goodbye to PayPal withdrawal restrictions and gain full access to your money—wherever you are. Whether you choose Wise or Payoneer, the key is knowing that you have options beyond PayPal’s limitations. Follow these simple steps to take back control of your funds today.
