Step-by-Step Guide to Sending Money

Getting started with sending money from InnBucks to EcoCash couldn't be easier! Here's a straightforward guide to walk you through the process:

  1. Open the InnBucks app on your device.
Select the "send money" option from the main menu.
  1. Choose Zipit as your payment method.
Tap on the "search bank" option.
  1. From the list of available banks, choose EcoCash.

Enter the amount you wish to send.
Confirm the transaction and you're done
