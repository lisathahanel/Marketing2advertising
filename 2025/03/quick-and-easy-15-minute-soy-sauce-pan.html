Delicious Soy Sauce Noodles in Just 15 Minutes

Short on time? Watch this brief video summary to quickly grasp the key points of this blog post

If you're looking for a quick and mouthwatering meal, you're in for a treat! These savory soy sauce pan-fried noodles take less than 15 minutes to make and are sure to satisfy your taste buds. Whether you're vegetarian or want to add chicken or beef, this dish is easily adaptable

Gather Your Ingredients

For this simple recipe, you will need the following ingredients:

  • 450 g cooked noodles
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2.5 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 4 tbsp water or vegetable stock
  • 6 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 3 green onions, cut
  • Optional: chopped red chili for heat

Easy Cooking Instructions

Start by mixing soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sesame oil, water, and sugar in a bowl until well blended. Cook the noodles as per the package instructions, then drain and set them aside.

In a pan, drizzle a bit of oil and heat it over medium-high. Once hot, add the garlic, the white part of the green onions, and if you dare, the chopped red chili. Sauté for 2-3 minutes, allowing the garlic to become fragrant.

Next, add your cooked noodles and pour the flavorful sauce over them. Mix well, ensuring all the noodles are coated. Turn the heat to high, toss in some bean sprouts, and sauté for another 2-3 minutes.

Finally, add the remaining green onions and sauté for an additional 1-2 minutes. Your delicious soy sauce noodles are now ready to be enjoyed. Happy cooking
