Getting Started with InnBucks
The InnBucks app is designed with user-friendliness in mind. To begin your transfer process, ensure that you have the app downloaded on your device and that you have created an account. Once your account is set up, you can utilize the app's features, including the Zipit service, to send money securely and quickly.

Steps to Send Money from InnBucks to EcoCash
Follow these simple steps to initiate a money transfer from InnBucks to EcoCash:

Open your InnBucks app and navigate to the “Send Money” option.

Select Zipit as your preferred payment method. This service enables secure and efficient transactions.

Tap the “Search Bank” option to find available financial institutions.

From the list that appears, choose EcoCash, which is one of Zimbabwe's most popular mobile money services.

Enter the amount you wish to send. Make sure to double-check the details to avoid any mistakes.

Confirm your transaction by following the prompts. It’s important to ensure all information is correct before finalizing the transfer.

Once confirmed, your loved ones will receive the funds directly into their EcoCash account almost instantly. This ensures that they can access the money quickly, no matter where they are in Zimbabwe.
