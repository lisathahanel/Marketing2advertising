Priscilla Zawedde, popularly known as Azawi, is a Ugandan singer-songwriter and dancer whose vibrant Afrobeats sound blends Luganda and English. Signed to Swangz Avenue in 2019, she has quickly risen to international acclaim—becoming the first female Ugandan artist to grace the iconic billboards of Times Square in New York City and Los Angeles.
