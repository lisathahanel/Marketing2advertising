How to Use:

1. Place the machine on a stable, heat-resistant surface.

2. Add dry popcorn kernels to the inner chamber using the provided measuring cup.

3. Cover with the transparent lid and ensure the outlet faces a bowl.

4. Press the power button to start the machine.

5. Wait until popping finishes, then unplug and serve immediately.

Tips and Recommendations:

- Do not use oil or butter inside the chamber.

- Clean only after the unit has fully cooled.

- Wipe interior with a dry, soft cloth.

- Allow 10 minutes of rest before repeating another batch.

- Store in a cool, ventilated area away from moisture.
