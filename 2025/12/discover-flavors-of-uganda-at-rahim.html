Located in the heart of Uganda, Rahim Foods Restaurant is a culinary haven that serves up a diverse range of delicious dishes. Our menu showcases the rich flavors of Ugandan cuisine, alongside popular fast food favorites.

Our Story

At Rahim Foods, we're passionate about serving food that not only tantalizes your taste buds but also showcases the rich cultural heritage of Uganda. Our chefs use only the freshest ingredients to create mouth-watering dishes that will leave you wanting more.

Our Menu

Our menu features a wide range of Ugandan classics, including:

- Matooke: a staple dish made from steamed or boiled green bananas

- Millet flour (kalo): a traditional Ugandan staple made from millet flour

- Posho (ugali): a thick cornmeal porridge

- Cassava, sweet potatoes, yams, and pumpkin: delicious and nutritious root vegetables

- Spinach and collard greens: sautéed to perfection with a hint of spices

- Beef and steamed meat in banana leaves (luwombo): tender and flavorful meat dishes

We also serve up popular fast food favorites, including:

- Chips: crispy fries cooked to perfection

- Chicken: finger-licking good chicken dishes

- Pilau rice: flavorful rice dishes cooked with spices and herbs

Our Signature Platters

Our legendary Local Food Lusaniya and Fast Food Lusaniya platters are a must-try. These generous platters offer a taste of the best of Ugandan cuisine and fast food, all in one sitting.
