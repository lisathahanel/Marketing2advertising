✅ Tips on How to Use These Sites Effectively

  • Post clear photos and detailed, honest descriptions — products or services with good descriptions get more attention.

  • Pick the right category and location (e.g. Kampala, Wakiso) — targeted ads tend to perform better.

  • For services or B2B businesses, use business-directory sites (like Business Pages Uganda or Yellow Uganda) to build credibility.

  • Consider listing on multiple platforms — classifieds + directory + niche site — to maximize reach.

  • Monitor responses frequently (calls/messages) to avoid missing potential customers
