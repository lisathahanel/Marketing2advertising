2026 Top 12 Websites to Advertise Your Business in Uganda

Updated for 2026 · Free & Paid Options · Classifieds + Directories

If you want to advertise your business in Uganda in 2026, these are the platforms that still deliver real results. From free classifieds to professional business directories, this list covers the sites Ugandans actually use to buy, sell, and find services.

1. Free Classifieds & Marketplaces

These platforms are perfect for quick visibility — products, services, jobs, rentals, and second-hand items.

1. Jiji.ug

Uganda’s leading online marketplace. List electronics, furniture, vehicles, property, services, and jobs for free. Extremely high traffic and trusted by millions of users.

2. Ttunda Classifieds Uganda

Simple and popular free classifieds site. Great for selling items, offering services, rentals, and job postings. Easy to use and widely searched.

3. Afribaba Uganda

Local classifieds platform focused on Uganda. Useful for goods, services, rentals, and reaching buyers across the country.

4. Owino Market Online

Free advertising platform inspired by the famous Owino market. Ideal for services, second-hand goods, and local buyers looking for bargains.

5. NanzDeals

Free classifieds site operating in Uganda and other East African countries. Post goods, services, real estate, and more.

6. BambaAd

Classifieds site where you can list cars, real estate, appliances, rentals, and a wide range of products and services.

7. Pundas Marketplace

Growing classifieds and marketplace platform for Uganda. Good for electronics, services, property, and everyday items.

2. Business Directories & Listing Platforms

Best for formal businesses, service providers, and anyone who wants long-term credibility and B2B exposure.

8. Business Pages Uganda

One of the largest business directories in the country. Ideal for formal listings that help suppliers, partners, and clients find you.

9. Yellow Uganda

Verified business directory and search portal. Excellent for SMEs that want to build trust and improve discoverability.

10. East Africa Tenders

Directory with strong business listings across Uganda. Useful if you offer services or want to connect with B2B clients and tenders.

11. Diraplate

Listing directory covering hospitality, transport, and many other sectors. Niche but valuable depending on your industry.

3. Sector-Specific Portal

12. Lamudi.co.ug

The go-to real-estate portal in Uganda. If you deal in houses, land, or rentals, this is one of the strongest platforms for property advertising.

Tips to Get Better Results

  • Use clear, high-quality photos and honest, detailed descriptions — good listings get far more responses.
  • Always select the correct category and location (Kampala, Wakiso, etc.) for better targeting.
  • For services or B2B, combine classifieds with directory listings (Business Pages Uganda or Yellow Uganda) to build credibility.
  • List on multiple platforms — classifieds + directory + niche site — to maximize reach.
  • Check messages and calls frequently so you don’t miss potential customers.

These 12 platforms remain some of the most effective ways to advertise a business in Uganda in 2026. Start with the free classifieds for quick visibility, then strengthen your presence with the directories for long-term trust and discovery.
