Our vision is to become Africa's premier auto repair center, setting the industry standard for excellence and building a trusted community of loyal customers through expertise, integrity, and dedication.

Founded in 2021, Pamtech Autoland was born out of a deep love for all things automotive. Our Founder, Engr. Chidomere Ndubuisi, MNSE, envisioned a place where car owners could experience top-notch service, unmatched expertise, and a welcoming atmosphere. 

With a team of dedicated technicians, Pamtech Autoland grew to become a trusted name in the automotive community. Today, we proudly serve a diverse and loyal customer base that trusts us for our unwavering commitment to quality service delivery
