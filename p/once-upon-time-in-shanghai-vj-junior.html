Mu Once Upon a Time in Shanghai, omukozi ow’amaanyi ava mu kyalo agenda mu kibuga Shanghai nga alina essuubi ery’okufuuka omugagga n’okukyusa obulamu bwe. A poor laborer travels to Shanghai with dreams of becoming rich but soon finds himself trapped in a violent criminal underworld. Mu mbeera eno, alina okukozesa obukugu bwe bwa kung fu okusobola okwerwanako n’okuwona mu kibuga ekijjudde obumenyi bw’amateeka.

💡 Kung Fu Action Highlight: Once Upon a Time in Shanghai erimu intense martial arts fights, street battles, n’olugendo lw’omusajja alwana okufuna obwenkanya mu kibuga ekijjudde obulabe. 🥋🔥

Nga bwe yeeyongera okuyingira mu lutalo n’abamenyi b’amateeka, amaanyi n’obuvumu bwe bifuuka ekintu kyokka ekimusobozesa okusigala ng’ali mulamu. As rival gangs rise against him, he must rely on his martial arts mastery and courage to fight powerful enemies. Mu mbeera eno, tulaba engeri omuntu omu bw’asobola okulwanyisa obubi n’okukuuma ekitiibwa kye mu nsi ey’obulabe.
