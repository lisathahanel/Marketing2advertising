Kim Kardashian's love life has been high profile and widely reported over nearly three decades, including early marriages, very public relationships with athletes and entertainers, and recent romancing speculation.

Early Relationships & Marriage +

😊

  • Damon Thomas (2000–2003): Kardashian’s first marriage at age 19 to a music producer. They divorced after about 3 years.
  • Ray J (≈2003–2006): After her first divorce, she dated singer Ray J. Their relationship later became widely known because of a sex tape filmed in 2003 and released in 2007.

Mid-Career Public Relationships +

💕

  • Nick Lachey & Nick Cannon (circa 2006–2007): Short-lived dating links with both singers around the mid-2000s.
  • Reggie Bush (2007–2010): The NFL star was one of her longest early public relationships; they were on-off for several years.
  • Miles Austin (2010) and Gabriel Aubry (2010): Briefly connected relationships reported in 2010.

High-Profile Marriages and Major Relationship +

💍

  • Kris Humphries (2010–2011): Dated, got engaged, and married the NBA player. Their marriage famously lasted 72 days before Kardashian filed for divorce.
  • Kanye West (2012–2021): One of her most public relationships. They began dating in 2012, married in 2014 in Italy, had four children (North, Saint, Chicago, and Psalm), and officially divorced in 2022 after media-intense years together. 😂

Recent & Rumored Relationships +

🔥

  • Pete Davidson (2021–2022): After hosting Saturday Night Live, Kardashian and the comedian had a widely covered romance that lasted about nine months.
  • Odell Beckham Jr. (2023–2024): Reportedly involved in a brief, mostly private relationship that fizzled out by spring 2024.
  • Rumored & Current (2025–2026): Recently there have been rumors and sightings linking Kardashian with Formula 1 champion Lewis Hamilton — from weekend getaways to appearances together at major events like Super Bowl LX — but neither has publicly confirmed an official relationship.
