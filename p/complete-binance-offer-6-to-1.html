This guide walks you through the simple process of completing the Binance offer via JumpTask. Follow the steps below to create your Binance account, complete KYC verification, deposit funds, and perform your required SPOT trades successfully.

Quick Steps Overview:
Step 1: Take the Offer
Step 2: Register
Step 3: Pass Verification
Step 4: Deposit
Step 5: Complete SPOT Trade

Step 1: Take the Offer

Click the "Take the Offer" button on the Binance offer inside JumpTask. This will redirect you to the official Binance website.

👉 Click Here to Take the Offer

Step 2: Register on Binance

Choose Registration Method

  • Click Register
  • Use Email, Phone Number, Apple, or Google account

Verify Your Account

  • Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email/phone
  • Submit within 30 minutes

Create a Secure Password

  • Minimum 8 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter
  • At least one number

🎉 Congratulations! Your Binance account is created.

Step 3: Complete Verification (KYC)

  • Go to Profile → Identification
  • Click Get Verified
  • Enter accurate personal information
  • Upload your ID documents
  • Complete the face verification (liveness check)

Binance will review your submission. You’ll receive an email once approved. If rejected multiple times, wait 24 hours before retrying.

Step 4: Deposit Funds

  • Open Binance App
  • Tap Deposit
  • Select Deposit Crypto (or FIAT)
  • Choose cryptocurrency (e.g., BNB)
  • Select the correct network ⚠️
  • Copy deposit address and send funds

⚠️ Selecting the wrong network may result in permanent loss of funds.

Step 5: Complete SPOT Trade

  1. Go to Trade → Spot
  2. Select a trading pair (e.g., BNB/USDT)
  3. Choose Market (instant) or Limit
  4. Enter trade amount and confirm

Market orders execute instantly. Limit orders execute when price conditions are met.
