🍕 Chicken Pizza Recipe | How To Make Pizza At Home (Easy & Cheesy)

Learn how to make easy homemade chicken pizza with a soft, chewy crust, juicy pan-fried chicken, and loads of melted mozzarella cheese. This simple pizza recipe requires minimal kneading and delivers restaurant-quality results right from your kitchen!

✨ Why You’ll Love This Recipe:

✔ Minimal kneading required

✔ Soft and chewy crust

✔ Loaded with cheesy goodness

✔ Perfect for beginners

🛒 Ingredients

For Pizza Dough

3/4 Cup (180ml) Lukewarm Water

2 Teaspoons Sugar

1 Teaspoon (3g) Instant Yeast (or Active Dry Yeast)

1 Tablespoon Olive Oil

2 Cups (250g) All Purpose Flour

1 Teaspoon Salt

For Chicken

220g Boneless Chicken Breast/Thighs (cubed)

Salt to taste

1 Teaspoon Ginger Garlic Paste

1 Teaspoon Lemon Juice

2 Tablespoons Thick Yogurt

1 Teaspoon Coriander Powder

1/2 Teaspoon Cumin Powder

1 1/2 Teaspoon Paprika

1/2 Teaspoon Black Pepper

🍕 Toppings

3 Tablespoons Pizza Sauce

150g Mozzarella Cheese (shredded)

Fried Chicken Cubes

Onions & Bell Peppers (cubed)

Basil/Oregano & Chili Flakes (optional)

Olive Oil (optional)

🔥 Pro Tip:

Always use fresh mozzarella and avoid overloading toppings to get a perfectly crispy crust.

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Method

Step 1: Prepare ingredients by measuring flour, yeast, and water accurately.

Step 2: Mix sugar with lukewarm water, add yeast, and stir. If using active dry yeast, let it rest for 10 minutes until frothy.

Step 3: Add olive oil and half of the flour. Mix, then add remaining flour gradually. Knead for 5–10 minutes.

💡 Tip:

The dough should be soft and slightly sticky. Avoid adding too much flour!

Step 4: Form a smooth ball, grease lightly, cover, and let it rise until doubled (35–90 mins).

Step 5: Divide dough into 2 portions and spread on a floured surface or parchment paper.

Step 6: Keep edges slightly thick and poke holes to prevent puffing.

Step 7: Spread pizza sauce, add cheese, chicken, veggies, and more cheese on top.

Step 8: Bake in a preheated oven at 230°C (446°F) for 10–11 minutes.

Step 9: Garnish with oregano, basil, and chili flakes. Serve hot.

🍽️ Serving Tip:

Brush crust edges with olive oil before baking for a golden crispy finish. Skip if you prefer soft crust.

⏱️ Baking Details

Temperature: 230°C / 446°F

Time: 10–11 minutes

Yield: 2 medium (10-inch) pizzas
