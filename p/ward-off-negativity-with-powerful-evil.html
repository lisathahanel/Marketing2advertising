What is the Evil Eye Charm?

The Evil Eye charm, also known as the Nazar or the "Eye of Horus," has a rich history spanning cultures and civilizations. It is often depicted as a blue or turquoise eye-shaped amulet, believed to possess mystical powers that deflect harmful intentions and ill wishes.
