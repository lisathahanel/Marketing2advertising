A rule-following college freshman on a strict scholarship from an abstinence group makes one mistake: she gets drunk for the first time in her life during a chaotic campus party. The night spirals, and her embarrassing antics are secretly recorded by a shady video producer hoping to profit from her slip-up.

When she discovers the footage could cost her everything — her scholarship, her reputation, and her future — she teams up with her friends for a cross-country mission to track the producer down and reclaim the video before it goes public.

Part comedy, part adventure, and part coming-of-age story, this fast-paced chase explores friendship, consequences, and learning to take control of your own story.
