🥟 Samosa Pockets Recipe | How to Make Crispy Samosa Pastry at Home

Learn how to make crispy samosa pockets pastry at home with this easy step-by-step recipe. Perfect for homemade samosas, this dough is flaky, crunchy, and beginner-friendly using simple ingredients.

🔥 Tips for Perfect Samosa Pockets

✔ Always rest the dough before rolling

✔ Roll evenly for uniform cooking

✔ Seal edges properly to avoid oil leakage

✔ Fry on medium heat for crispy golden texture
