Nga bwe batambula mu mbeera z’ebizibu, alina okuba ekifaananyi mu byobulimba by’eby’obulamu. He becomes the star of his own story, blurring the line between observer and participant. Obwesige bwe n’emyoyo gy’abantu abalala byeyongera okuba mu kkomo ly’okubuuza obulamu n’obulimba.

Mu nkomerero, lutalo lwa buli kimu luba mu kkomo ly’okulaba, okutegeera n’okufuna obulamu bw’amaanyi mu nsi ejjudde obulabe. Through manipulation, cunning, and ambition, he navigates a dark world where ethics and ambition collide. Obulamu bwe bujja kuba ku mukono gw’ebyobulimba n’obukugu bw’omu nsi y’obutaliimu.
