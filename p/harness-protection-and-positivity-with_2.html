Deciphering the Symbolism Behind the Hamsa Hand

The Hamsa hand is a symbol steeped in history, with its origins tracing back to ancient civilizations in the Middle East and North Africa. Often depicted as an open right hand, the Hamsa is recognized as a sign of protection that also represents blessings, power, and strength. The eye at the centre of the palm is traditionally believed to shield against the 'evil eye', a malevolent glare that is said to bring bad luck to the unsuspecting.

Exploring the numerous interpretations across different cultures, the Hamsa hand is known by various names such as the Hand of Fatima in Islam, the Hand of Miriam in Judaism, and the Hand of Mary in Christianity. Each tradition imbues the symbol with its unique spiritual significance while maintaining its core purpose as a protective amulet.
